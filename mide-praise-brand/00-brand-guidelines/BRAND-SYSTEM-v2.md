## 0. Core creative principle

**[LOCKED]** *Spiritual fire expressed through elegant simplicity.*

Everything in this system — colour, type, photography, motion, merchandise, website — must pass this test. If it is loud without clarity, ornate without purpose, or decorative without meaning, it is out.

## 2. Visual direction

### 2.1 Mood [LOCKED]

| In | Out |
|---|---|
| Warm, grounded, reverent | Cold tech / cyan neon |
| Quiet intensity | Loud spectacle without meaning |
| Crafted and intentional | Generic gospel stock aesthetic |
| Light as presence | Pure black void as default |
| Earth, wine, ember, gold | Purple-on-white AI defaults; cream + terracotta cliché stacks |

### 2.2 Texture & atmosphere [LOCKED]

Prefer **atmosphere over flat colour**: soft grain, warm light falloff, subtle paper/cloth texture on print, restrained light bloom on digital hero moments. Gradients and patterns must serve hierarchy — never decorate emptiness.

### 2.3 Photography direction [PROPOSED]

- Real moments of worship, craft and community — not generic purple stock people.
- Prefer warm practical light, candle/stage ember, golden hour outdoors.
- Faces and hands in honest moments; avoid over-posed “ministry brochure” stares.
- Authorised photography only — rights and credits **[TBD]**.

## 3. Colour system

### 3.1 Core palette [LOCKED for logo exploration]

| Token | Name | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `brand.wine` | Deep Wine | `#301821` | Primary brand / dark surfaces / logo exploration base |
| `brand.ember` | Ember | `#D95732` | Accent fire — CTAs, emphasis, spiritual heat |
| `brand.gold` | Antique Gold | `#C4A15B` | Sacred highlight — secondary accent, awards, fine detail |
| `surface.ivory` | Warm Ivory | `#F7F1E7` | Light surfaces / print ground |
| `text.charcoal` | Charcoal | `#292529` | Body text on light |
| `border.soft` | Soft Neutral | `#E4DACE` | Borders, hairlines, quiet structure |
| `brand.ember-deep` | Ember Deep | `#B8431F` | Pressed / deep ember |
| `brand.gold-deep` | Gold Deep | `#7A5C1E` | Deep gold / metallic print reference |

### 3.2 Usage rules [LOCKED]

- Start in neutrals (`ivory`, `charcoal`, `wine`). Add **one** brand accent per composition unless hierarchy demands gold + ember together for sacred emphasis.
- Ember is the primary action colour. Gold is ceremonial — do not use gold for every button.
- Do not invent new brand hues without a decision. Semantic colours (success / warning / danger / info) may exist in product UI but are not brand expression.
- Test contrast separately on wine and on ivory. Dark mode is not the inverse of light — design it.

### 3.3 Contrast targets [LOCKED]

- Body text ≥ **4.5:1** against its surface.
- Large text / UI components ≥ **3:1**.
- Do not use “minimalism” as an excuse for low contrast.

### 3.4 Pairings that work [PROPOSED]

| Surface | Text | Accent |
|---|---|---|
| Ivory | Charcoal | Ember or Gold |
| Wine | Ivory | Ember or Gold |
| Charcoal | Ivory | Ember |

Avoid gold text on ivory for small sizes (contrast risk). Avoid ember on wine for small text without checking ratio.

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Principles [LOCKED]

- **Two faces maximum** for the public system (display + body). A third mono face is allowed for code / technical only.
- One modular type scale. Body face chosen first; display supports it.
- Line-height: body **1.4–1.6**; display **1.1–1.25**.
- Measure ~**70 characters** max for long body copy.
- Tabular numerals in data; proportional elsewhere.

### 4.2 Recommended pairing [PROPOSED — lock after logo]

| Role | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display | High-craft serif or refined humanist with spiritual weight | Must feel intentional, not “church flyer clipart” |
| Body | Clean readable sans with warmth | Avoid Inter / Roboto / Arial as the brand face |
| Optional accent | Small-caps or tracked label style for section markers | Use sparingly |

**[TBD]** Exact family names, weights, and licensing after logo direction is chosen so letterforms can relate.

### 4.3 Hierarchy [LOCKED]

Establish hierarchy by **size → weight → colour → position**. Never by piling decoration. Three levels per surface; more is noise.

## 6. Layout & composition

### 6.1 Spatial system [LOCKED]

- **4-point** base for dense digital tools; **8-point** for marketing / comfortable surfaces.
- One grid language; break it deliberately, never accidentally.
- Group with proximity before dividers or boxes.

### 6.2 Marketing / landing surfaces [LOCKED]

- First viewport reads as **one composition**, not a dashboard.
- Brand name is a **hero-level signal**, not only nav text.
- Prefer full-bleed atmospheric hero over inset media cards.
- Hero budget: brand, one headline, one supporting sentence, one CTA group, one dominant visual. No stats strips, schedule dumps, or promo stickers on the hero.
- No detached badges / floating chips on hero media.
- Cards only when they contain a real interaction; default is no cards.
- One job per section.

### 6.3 Product / dense surfaces [LOCKED]

Density is respect for people doing the work. Tools default compact; marketing may breathe.

## 7. Iconography & UI craft

### 7.1 Icons [LOCKED]

- One icon family, one weight across the product.
- Icons are vocabulary, not decoration.
- Pair with labels unless truly universal (search, close).

### 7.2 Affordances & feedback [LOCKED]

- Interactive elements look interactive.
- Every control ships: default, hover, focus, active, disabled — with disabled explained when used.
- Feedback within ~100ms for actions.
- Toasts confirm; they do not carry blocking errors.

### 7.3 Overlays [LOCKED]

Popover → sheet → modal (lightest that works). Modals for destructive or truly focused tasks only.

---

## 8. Motion

### 8.1 Principle [LOCKED]

Motion is a sentence. Every animation says origin, status, or connection. If it doesn’t speak, delete it.

### 8.2 Timing [LOCKED]

- Entrances / exits: **200–300ms**
- State nudges: **80–120ms**
- Ease-out entering; ease-in leaving
- Honour `prefers-reduced-motion` — fall back to fades or none

### 8.3 Jobs of motion [LOCKED]

Confirm · Anticipate · Reward (rare). Nothing else.

### 8.4 Sonic logo / brand sound [TBD]

Not defined. Do not invent a jingle without owner and music direction.

## 9. Voice & tone

### 9.1 Voice [PROPOSED]

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Reverent | Speaks of God and people with weight | Stiff, archaic, unreachable |
| Clear | Plain language, short sentences when it matters | Cold or clinical |
| Warm | Human, invitational | Soft without substance |
| Crafted | Care in every word | Overwritten poetry on every button |

### 9.2 Copy rules [LOCKED]

- Real product words from the first draft — no lorem after wireframes.
- Error messages name **cause + fix**.
- Microcopy carries brand more than an oversized logo.
- First delight is free — do not gate all value behind signup.

### 9.3 Formal title & bios [TBD]

## 16. Do / Don’t (quick reference)

### Do

- Lead with content and presence before chrome.
- One primary action per view.
- Keep fire quiet and intentional.
- Preserve `Mokúnfáyọ̀` spelling with diacritics.

### Don’t

- Ship an unapproved logo.
- Default to purple/cyan “AI gospel” aesthetics.
- Use lorem or fake bios.
- Hide important spiritual or booking actions behind mystery UI.
- Treat stakeholder taste as brand law without a JTBD or strategy reason.
